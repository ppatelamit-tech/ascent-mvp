Everyone sells you calm. This trains capacity.

Meditation apps lower the noise. Coaches tell you what they did. Neither measures anything, and neither has a structural model of what actually goes wrong in a founder. Ascent does: seven blocks, seven capacities, trained in order from survival to letting go.

A scoreboard, not a streak

A baseline Profile on day one. Six-week blocks that end in a re-measure. You see the delta, not a mood log.

Skills, not meditations

Real-world challenges with an evidence file. Thirty-second truth drills graded by a rubric. Status scenarios with a sparring partner. Values made explicit before motivation is rebuilt on them.

A human door

When the Profile or the scoreboard says training alone will not move it, Ascent says so and points to a clinician. That is the feature nobody else ships.

How a block works

Six weeks on one capacity. A daily practice you do, a weekly lesson you watch, a number that moves.

  1. Baseline

    Forty-two statements about the past month, one at a time. Twelve minutes. Seven readings and a recommendation on where to start.

  2. Train

    Capability: pick a challenge, rate your body before and after, write one line for the evidence file. Communication: a topic, thirty seconds, graded on six things.

  3. Re-measure

    At the end of the block the domain is scored again. The delta is the result. If two blocks move nothing, that is information too.

The two blocks in the pilot

The other five follow once these have been through their first hundred founders.

Capability · over fear

Confidence by evidence

Three challenges a day from the areas you choose: one Warm, one Load, one Edge. Talk to a stranger, make the cold call, enter the race you are not sure you can finish. Points per tier, levels from points, a weekly and all-time board. Each completion is a line in a file you read back when you do not feel confident. Confidence is a record, not a mood.

Communication · over lies

Say it in thirty seconds

"Tell the board the real number." "Tell your co-founder what you actually think." Thirty seconds, transcribed on your device, graded on whether the hard thing came first, how specific you were, how many hedges, whether you owned it, whether you finished, and what it cost you to say.

What this is not

Ascent is a training program, not medical care. It does not diagnose, it does not treat, and using it does not make you anyone's patient. If you are dealing with depression, anxiety, or anything that is pressing on your whole life at once, a clinician is the right first step, and Ascent will tell you that when the numbers say so.
